# Form & Flow Fitness – A/B Test Plan

**Website:** https://delaneybuzz.github.io/full-body-workouts/

**Page:** Homepage

### 1. What I Am Testing
I will test whether changing the homepage headline affects visitors' interest in exploring the workouts on my website.

- **Version A:** "Stronger Every Day. Your Way."
- **Version B:** "Find Your Next Workout: Simple Routines for Every Fitness Level"

The headline is the only element being changed. Everything else on the homepage will remain identical.

### 2. Hypothesis
I predict that Version B will result in a higher percentage of visitors wanting to explore the workouts. The new headline clearly describes what the website offers and may be more appealing to visitors looking for specific workout routines.

### 3. AI Visitors and Random Assignment
I will use ChatGPT GPT-6 (Instant) with the same settings for all trials. Each AI visitor will represent a beginner or intermediate fitness enthusiast looking for simple workouts.

I will conduct 100 trials, with 50 assigned to Version A and 50 assigned to Version B. I used Microsoft Excel to generate and randomly shuffle the assignments before testing. The randomized order was saved so it would not change during the experiment.

Every trial will take place in a new, unpersonalized Temporary Chat without previous trial context. I will use the same AI model and instructions throughout the experiment.

### 4. Exact AI Visitor Prompt
"You are a beginner or intermediate fitness enthusiast looking for simple and effective workout routines. Read the following homepage content carefully. Based only on this page, would you choose to explore the workouts offered on this website? Answer YES or NO, followed by one sentence explaining your decision.

Homepage content:
[Insert the complete text of the assigned version here.]"

The instructions will remain identical in every trial. Only the assigned homepage content will change.

### 5. Primary Outcome
The primary outcome will be whether the AI visitor answers YES or NO to exploring the workouts. I will record YES as 1 and NO as 0 and calculate the percentage of YES responses for each version.

### 6. Sample Size and Timeline
I will conduct 100 trials total, with 50 trials for Version A and 50 for Version B. I originally committed my A/B test plan on November 9, 2026, and plan to launch testing by November 17 and complete all trials by November 24, 2026.

I will collect all 100 responses without stopping early based on the results.

### 7. Analysis
I will compare the percentage of YES responses for Version A and Version B and calculate the difference in percentage points. My hypothesis will be supported if Version B has a higher YES percentage.

I will also use a two-proportion statistical test to evaluate whether the observed difference could be due to chance, using a 0.05 significance threshold. I will report the results whether or not they support my hypothesis.

### 8. SEO Protection
Version B is saved as index-b.html with a noindex meta tag and will not be included in the sitemap to prevent competition with the original homepage in search results.
