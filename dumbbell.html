Full Body Dumbbell Workout

One pair of dumbbells. Six effective exercises. A stronger you.

Your 30-Minute Dumbbell Workout

Dumbbell workouts are an excellent way to develop strength without needing a room full of equipment. Whether you're exercising at home or at the gym, this full-body routine trains your legs, chest, back, shoulders, and arms.

Workout Duration: Approximately 30 minutes

Equipment: One pair of dumbbells

Difficulty: Beginner to intermediate

Structure: 2 sets per exercise, 8–12 repetitions

Rest: 45–75 seconds between sets

The Exercises

1. Dumbbell Goblet Squat

Targets: Quadriceps, glutes, and core

Hold one dumbbell close to your chest. Keep your feet about shoulder-width apart, bend your knees and hips, and lower into a comfortable squat. Push through your feet to return to standing.

Sets and Reps: 2 × 10

2. Dumbbell Floor Press

Targets: Chest, shoulders, and triceps

Lie on your back with your knees bent and a dumbbell in each hand. Press the weights upward, then lower your upper arms gently toward the floor while keeping your wrists aligned.

Sets and Reps: 2 × 10

3. Bent-Over Dumbbell Row

Targets: Upper back, lats, and biceps

Hinge at your hips with your knees slightly bent. Keep your back neutral and pull the dumbbells toward your hips. Lower them slowly without swinging your torso.

Sets and Reps: 2 × 12

4. Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift

Targets: Hamstrings and glutes

Stand tall holding dumbbells in front of your thighs. Push your hips backward while keeping your knees slightly bent. Lower the weights close to your legs, then return to standing by driving your hips forward.

Sets and Reps: 2 × 10

5. Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Targets: Shoulders and triceps

Hold dumbbells at shoulder height with your palms facing forward or inward. Press upward without excessively arching your back, then lower with control.

Sets and Reps: 2 × 10

6. Dumbbell Hammer Curl

Targets: Biceps and forearms

Stand with your arms at your sides and palms facing inward. Curl the dumbbells toward your shoulders while keeping your elbows near your body. Lower slowly.

Sets and Reps: 2 × 12

Tips for Better Results

Choose dumbbells that make the final repetitions challenging while still allowing proper form. Begin with a lighter weight if you're learning a movement, and gradually increase resistance as your technique improves.

Warm up for three to five minutes before starting. Stop any movement that causes sharp pain, and allow recovery time between challenging sessions.
