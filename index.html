Why Full Body Training?

Full body workouts train multiple major muscle groups in one session, making them an efficient choice for busy schedules. By combining upper-body, lower-body, and core movements, you can develop balanced strength while keeping your routine manageable.

Our recommendation for beginners is to start with two or three strength sessions per week, allowing recovery between challenging workouts.
